A village reborn

You are the grandchild of the late Grandpa Hasan. With a bag and a few coins, you arrive in Sivrice, at the farm that has been waiting for years.

First you gather wood, braid rope and buy your first chickens. Then come fields, barns, a village fountain, a tea house and a guesthouse. As the village grows, the roads to the Northlands open, and in the end you win back your grandfather's mansion.

  1. Act 1

    Empty Hands

    Wood, rope, baskets and your first chickens. Learn to get by.

  2. Act 2

    Taking Root

    Barns, fields, workers, tourists and a diving centre.

  3. Act 3

    The Northlands

    Çamlıbel, the mill, the quarry, beekeeping and a school.

  4. Act 4

    The Great Legacy

    Irrigation, a museum, the lake festival and the family mansion.

What can you do?

  • 🌾

    Farming & animals

    Sow, water, harvest. Raise chickens, cows, horses and donkeys; expand your barn and coop.

  • 🏘️

    Build your village

    Fountain, tea house, guesthouse, museum. Meet the headman's requests and bring in tourists.

  • 🎣

    Lake & nature

    Fish and dive in Lake Hazar, forage mushrooms and fruit. Seasons and weather change.

  • 👥

    Multiplayer

    Visit friends' villages, form a co-op, trade at the market, talk on the regional radio.

  • 🧑‍🌾

    Workers

    Murat tends the animals, Ayşe the fields. They keep working even while you're offline.

  • 🚌

    A big world

    Take the bus to the city or a taxi to the Northlands. Villages, towns and hundreds of people.

About Tasami

Tasami is an independent studio that designs and develops games and builds mobile and web apps. It was founded by two chemical engineers.

We work the way engineers do: build the system first, then measure, test and improve. That is why the economy, production chains and world in our games fit together.

Our name comes from our founders' names: Taha and Sami.
