Story

A village reborn

You are the grandchild of the late Grandpa Hasan. With a bag and a few coins, you arrive in Sivrice, at the farm that has been waiting for you for years.

First you gather wood, braid rope and buy your first chickens. Then come fields, barns, pastures and a village fountain. As the village grows come a dairy and a co-op, then factories, trucks and export ships.

In the end you build your own holding company and pass Grandpa Hasan's legacy on to the village through a foundation.

6 chapters

From empty hands to a holding

  1. Chapter 1

    Empty Hands

    Wood, rope, baskets and your first chickens. Repair grandpa's house and learn to get by.

  2. Chapter 2

    Taking Root

    Barns, fields, your first workers, tourists and a diving centre on the lake.

  3. Chapter 3

    The Village's Hope

    The Northlands open up: milk and a dairy, a co-op, the bank and a price war with Demirkol.

  4. Chapter 4

    Factory Smoke

    Flour, feed and sugar factories, a textile workshop and contracts with wholesalers.

  5. Chapter 5

    Roads and Harbours

    A vocational school, scholarships, a truck fleet, cold storage and the first export ship.

  6. Chapter 6

    Legacy

    A holding and its companies, a hospital and library for the village, the Hasan Dede Foundation.

Features

A living world

01

A farm from scratch

Sow, water and harvest. Grow your farm step by step with barns, a coop, a greenhouse, irrigation and solar panels, and place your house, fields and barns wherever you like.

  • Fields & greenhouse
  • Free placement
  • Solar
02

Livestock

Cow, sheep and goat pastures; lambs, kids and Elif the vet. Make yoghurt, ayran, cheese, kashar and butter at your dairy.

  • Cows · sheep · goats
  • Dairy
  • Vet
03

A living village

Hundreds of villagers going about their day: tea house, mosque, guesthouse, tourists. Ask the night owls why they're still out.

  • 400+ villagers
  • Tourists
  • Village projects
04

From factory to harbour

Build flour, feed and sugar factories, sign wholesale contracts, haul goods by truck and export from the port of Mersin.

  • Factories
  • Logistics
  • Exports
05

Lake and nature

Fish Lake Hazar: big fish fight harder but sell for more. Find the waterfall on the northern slope, dive, forage mushrooms and be ready for the seasons and the wolves.

  • Fishing
  • Waterfall
  • Seasons
06

Play together

Visit friends' villages, form a co-op, trade at the market, talk on the regional radio or by voice call.

  • Co-op
  • Market
  • Radio

Latest updates

  1. Version 3.8028 Oct 2026

    Empty land is now accepted when placing buildings: buildings take up only their current size (space used to be reserved for the largest level), villagers' walking spots no longer block a spot and villagers walk around the new building. If an upgraded building no longer fits, it is moved to a free spot and you can choose its place.

  2. Version 3.7927 Oct 2026

    You can now build over grass and bushes; the plants under a new building are cleared (trees and rocks still block). While moving a building, all of its parts at the old spot (troughs, posts, water trough, sign) are hidden so the green area no longer looks blocked. The stable window no longer shows "Upgrade stable" twice.

  3. Version 3.7827 Oct 2026

    Building placement now checks overlaps properly: another house, a villager's house, yard walls, fences, roofs and trees show red and you can't build there. While you choose a spot, the building you're moving is hidden at its old place so the green area no longer looks like it sits on another building; it comes back if you cancel.
