Privacy policy

This policy covers the tasami.com.tr website. It also serves as our notice under Turkey's Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK). For the privacy policy of the game Legacy of the Land, see this page.

Data controller

Tasami (founders Taha and Sami). Contact: info@tasami.com.tr

What we collect

The site has no forms, sign-ups or accounts; we never ask for your name, email or similar details.

Visit statistics. We measure how many people visit the site and which pages they view with Cloudflare Web Analytics. It uses no cookies, does not identify you and only shows visits as totals.

Server logs. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may briefly log visitors' IP addresses for security. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google while loading them.

Why we collect it

We use visit statistics only to see which parts of the site people find interesting. We never use them for ads or any other purpose and never share them.

Old closed test sign-ups

Before the game launched on Google Play, the site had a closed test sign-up form. Now that the game is public we have removed it. The email addresses and names left through the form are no longer used and, as promised, we are deleting them. If you want confirmation that yours is gone, just write to us.

Cookies and data stored in your browser

The site has no advertising or tracking cookies. Your browser's local storage only keeps a few small preferences: whether you turned the music on or off, whether you have seen the intro animation this session, and whether you dismissed the cookie notice. You can delete all of this in your browser settings at any time.

Your rights

You have the right to know whether we process your personal data, to get information about it, to know its purpose and who it is shared with, to have it corrected, deleted or destroyed, to have those changes passed on to anyone it was shared with, to object to automated decisions against you and to claim compensation for unlawful processing.

To use these rights, just write to info@tasami.com.tr. We reply within 30 days.

Changes

When we update this policy we also change the date at the bottom.
